# Privacy Policy - Adam Elite TikTok Publishing **Last Updated:** November 3, 2026 ## 1. Introduction Adam Elite ("we," "us," "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and share information when you use our TikTok content publishing service ("Service"). ## 2. Information We Collect ### Information You Provide: - **TikTok Account Information**: Username, display name, profile picture (avatar) - **Content Preferences**: Video titles, descriptions, hashtags you choose ### Information Collected Automatically: - **Authentication Tokens**: TikTok access tokens and refresh tokens - **Video Metadata**: Upload timestamps, video IDs, publish status ### Information from TikTok API: - Basic profile data (via user.info.basic scope) - Video publish status (via video.publish scope) ## 3. How We Use Your Information We use your information to: - Authenticate your TikTok account via OAuth 2.0 - Publish content to your TikTok account - Track video upload and publish status - Improve the Service ## 4. Information Sharing We do NOT: - Sell your personal information - Share your data with third parties for marketing - Store your TikTok password We DO: - Store OAuth tokens locally for session persistence - Log API interactions for debugging purposes - Share anonymized usage statistics ## 5. Data Storage and Security - OAuth tokens are stored as encrypted strings in environment variables - Video metadata is stored in `state/proposals/` and `projects/tiktok/` - All data is stored locally within the Adam Elite workspace ## 6. Your Rights - **Access**: Request information about your data - **Correction**: Update your profile information - **Deletion**: Revoke app access in TikTok settings to stop authentication - **Portability**: Export your data from the workspace ## 7. Cookies and Tracking We do not use cookies or third-party tracking. The Service operates server-side only. ## 8. Children's Privacy The Service is not intended for users under 13. ## 9. Changes to Privacy Policy We may update this policy. Changes are effective immediately. ## 10. Contact For privacy questions: admin@adamelite.dev --- **By using this Service, you consent to the data practices described in this Privacy Policy.**
